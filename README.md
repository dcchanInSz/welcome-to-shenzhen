## English

### What this is

A practical handbook for delegates and visitors: what to eat, where to stay, how to get around, what to see, and what to expect during meeting week. The site is bilingual — use **EN / 中文** in the top-right corner.

This is an independent guide, **not an official publication**. Confirm important arrangements with government and APEC official channels.
