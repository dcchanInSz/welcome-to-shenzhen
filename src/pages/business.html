产业地图

深圳靠什么赚钱

深圳的产业结构有一个共同点：研发、供应链和量产在同一个城市半径内完成。这是它与大多数科技城市最大的区别。

电子信息与半导体

深圳的看家产业，从芯片设计、面板、模组到整机装配一应俱全。2025 年集成电路产量 747.65 亿块，电子计算机整机 4587.15 万台，服务器 230.44 万台。南山、龙华、坪山是设计与制造的主要承载区。

新能源汽车与智能网联

2025 年汽车制造业增加值增长 29.4%，是全市增速最快的工业门类之一。整车、电池、电机电控、智能座舱与自动驾驶算法在深圳及周边形成完整链条。坪山、深汕特别合作区是重要基地。

无人机与低空经济

2025 年民用无人机产量 745.38 万架，同比增长 40.1%。深圳是全球消费级无人机的研发与制造中心，同时在做低空物流、巡检、载人飞行器的适航与场景试点。南山、龙岗是主要聚集地。

人工智能与算力

从芯片、服务器到模型与应用，深圳的 AI 产业依附在硬件供应链上。2025 年服务器产量增长 29.5%，信息传输、软件和信息技术服务业增加值超过 5200 亿元。算力基础设施和政策支持是各区招商的重点。

医疗器械与生物医药

深圳的医疗器械以监护、影像、体外诊断和植介入耗材见长，产业链配套成熟。坪山是生物医药的核心承载区，光明科学城侧重基础研究与大型科研装置，南山聚集了一批高值耗材与诊断企业。

金融与前海

2025 年金融业增加值 5261.56 亿元，增长 12.1%。前海深港现代服务业合作区集中了跨境金融、融资租赁、供应链金融与外资机构，是深圳金融开放度最高的片区。

跨境电商

依托港口、机场与成熟的供应链，深圳是全国跨境电商卖家与物流服务商最密集的城市之一。2025 年限额以上单位网络零售额占商品零售额比重超过一半，前海、宝安、龙岗都有跨境电商产业园。

工业设计与时尚

深圳是联合国教科文组织认定的「设计之都」，工业设计、珠宝、钟表、眼镜、服装与家具产业基础深厚。水贝的黄金珠宝、大浪的服装、横岗的眼镜都形成了完整的产业带。

文化创意

游戏、数字内容、影视制作与创意设计是深圳文创的主力。南山科技园、华侨城创意文化园（OCT-LOFT）与龙岗数字创意产业走廊聚集了大量内容与设计团队。

会议设施

三类场地，三种用法

  • 深圳国际会展中心位于宝安区，是超大型展馆，适合几万至几十万平方米的展览、大型发布会与产业展。距市中心较远，参会者通常住在宝安或乘地铁前往。
  • 深圳会展中心位于福田中心区，紧邻地铁会展中心站，适合中型展览、行业论坛与配套边会。周边酒店与餐饮密度全市最高，步行即可完成会务与用餐。
  • 深圳国际交流中心位于福田香蜜湖北区，2026 年 7 月建成启用，具备会议中心、配套酒店与园林会议区，为国际高规格峰会和政务商务活动设计，是 APEC 主场馆所在片区。
  • 酒店会议厅福田、南山的高星级酒店普遍设有可分割的宴会厅与董事会议室，适合 20—300 人的商务会议，含餐饮与茶歇打包服务，通常最省事。
  • 档期建议11 月中下旬是深圳会展旺季，叠加 APEC 会议周，场地与酒店异常紧张。建议提前 2—3 个月锁定场地，并把住宿一并谈定。
  • 配套服务会展中心与酒店一般可提供同声传译设备、翻译人员、签到系统、直播与搭建服务，但需提前预订，临时安排价格高且不一定有位。
一条实用经验

如果会议规模在 50 人以内，优先考虑高星级酒店的董事会议室而不是会展中心的小会议室。酒店把会议室、茶歇、午餐、住宿打包在一起，你在会议周省下的协调时间远比差价值钱。

商务礼仪

九个动作，决定第一印象

深圳的商务文化是中国内地最不讲究繁文缛节的一类：效率优先、层级扁平。但基础礼仪做对了，沟通成本会明显下降。

  1. 准时，并且早到五分钟

    准时是最基本的尊重。提前 5 分钟到达会议室，留出交换名片和寒暄的时间。如果会迟到，务必提前发消息说明预计到达时间——深圳人对迟到本身宽容，对不通知不宽容。

  2. 称谓与名片

    双手递出、双手接收名片，接到后花两秒钟读一下再收好，不要随手塞进口袋或在上面写字。名片刻有职务，优先用「姓氏 + 职务」称呼，如「李总」「王经理」；不确定时用「先生 / 女士 + 姓氏」最安全。

  3. 会议节奏

    寒暄短、进入正题快。前五分钟可以聊天气、航班、上一次来访，之后直接讲议题。议程之外的闲谈通常放在餐桌上，而不是会议室里。

  4. 决策风格

    深圳企业决策偏数据驱动、偏快。用数字、时间表和责任人说事，比用品牌故事有效得多。对方如果说「我们研究一下」，通常意味着需要内部再走一轮流程，可在三天后礼貌跟进。

  5. 关系与信任

    深圳是一座移民城市，人脉靠合作积累而非血缘。首次见面谈事、第二次见面谈人、第三次才谈长期合作是常见的节奏。持续、守约、交付及时比一顿豪华晚宴更能建立信任。

  6. 送礼禁忌

    不送钟表（与「送终」谐音）、不送伞（与「散」谐音）、不送白色或菊花主题的物品、不送刀具。带本国的特产、企业纪念品、高品质文具或茶叶都很安全。礼物双手递上，对方通常不当面拆开。

  7. 酒桌与宴请

    深圳的酒桌文化比内地许多城市温和，多数场合以茶代酒完全可接受。如果有人敬酒，起身、杯子略低于对方是礼貌；不能喝时直接说明身体原因并举起茶杯，通常不会被为难。切勿酒驾，深圳查处极严。

  8. 公筷与分餐

    中餐厅通常会提供公筷公勺。取菜用公筷，自己的筷子不要伸进公盘翻找。部分餐厅按位上菜（分餐制），跟随主人或服务员的安排即可。吃饭时不要用筷子插在饭上，也不要敲碗。

  9. 着装

    正式商务场合穿深色西装、衬衫与皮鞋，不必打领带但备一条更稳妥。深圳气候湿热，商务休闲（有领衬衫 + 西裤或及膝裙）在多数写字楼场合被接受。空调偏冷，随身带外套。

宴请实务

怎么点菜，怎么落座

点菜

  • 让主人点菜，客人可以补充一两个偏好或忌口，不要抢着主导。
  • 人数与菜数大致相当，再加一汤一主食；深圳餐厅菜量偏大，初次点菜宁可少点。
  • 先问忌口：不吃辣、素食、清真、海鲜过敏、宗教信仰，问清楚比事后补救容易得多。
  • 有当地特色时优先点一两道，比如粤式烧腊、清蒸海鱼、砂锅粥或早茶点心。
  • 用公筷分餐；对方夹菜给你是礼貌，不必拒绝，吃不完可以先放着。
  • 买单通常由邀请方完成，客人强行买单可能被视作不给面子；可以先表达感谢，下次回请。

座次（圆桌）

  • 正对门口的位置通常是主位，由主人或最高职务者落座。
  • 主位右手边为第一主宾位，左手边为第二主宾位。客人等主人示意后再落座。
  • 主人一般坐在主位对面或靠近服务员出菜口的一侧，方便招呼上菜与买单。
  • 如有译员，通常安排在客人斜后方或相邻位置，方便低声翻译。
  • 不确定坐哪里时，站在座位旁等主人安排，这是最不会出错的做法。

差旅实务

会前三天要处理的事

住哪儿

谈金融与政务住福田；谈科技与互联网住南山；需要频繁往来香港住罗湖或前海。会议周福田与南山的高星级酒店最紧张，可退改的房型优先。参考 住宿指南。

通勤与限行

深圳对非深号牌小汽车实行工作日高峰时段限行，外地车辆需注意规定时段与范围。工作日早高峰（约 7:30—9:00）与晚高峰（约 17:30—19:30）主要干道拥堵明显，跨区会议建议把通勤时间按导航估算再加 40%。

停车

写字楼与商场普遍有地下停车场，首小时费用约 10—15 元，核心区更高。热门商圈与写字楼在上午 9 点后车位紧张。会议周会场周边通常取消临时停车，请优先选择地铁。

机场与高铁

宝安国际机场地铁 11 号线可直达福田，全程约 50 分钟；也可乘机场快线或打车，高峰期约 40—70 分钟。高铁主要停靠深圳北站（龙华）与福田站，后者位于市中心。参考 交通出行。

打印与商务中心

高星级酒店普遍设有商务中心，可打印、装订、扫描与临时租用会议室，按页或按小时收费。街边文印店价格更低、速度更快，写字楼集中的片区几乎每栋楼下都有。

翻译与同传

会展中心、国际交流中心与多数高星级酒店可提供同声传译设备与译员，需提前预订。小型会议用「外籍人士 + 母语译员 + 主要参会方」的三方模式往往比同传更高效。

名片印刷

建议在本国印好中英文对照的名片，中文名与职务请让母语者核对。深圳本地也可快速加印，写字楼周边的文印店通常当天可取。

网络与演示

会场 Wi-Fi 通常需要手机号验证，重要演示建议自带热点与离线版文件。投影接口以 HDMI 为主，Type-C 直连越来越常见，但仍应自带转换头。

时间预期

深圳人约会普遍准时，但对「路上堵车」有共识。跨区会议的最短可靠提前量：福田内部 30 分钟，福田到南山 45 分钟，福田到宝安 60 分钟。

创业与落地

如果要在深圳设点

公司注册与外资企业

外商投资企业的设立流程已大幅简化，可通过「一网通办」在线提交，多数材料线上办理。需要准备投资方主体资格证明、法定代表人身份文件、注册地址证明等，部分材料需公证认证并翻译。

前海等片区对港资、外资企业设有专门的服务窗口与绿色通道。具体流程以深圳市市场监督管理局与各区政务服务中心的最新指引为准。

税收优惠与产业政策

前海深港现代服务业合作区对符合《企业所得税优惠目录》的产业企业减按 15% 征收企业所得税，河套、光明科学城等片区也有各自的产业扶持与研发资助政策。

优惠目录、适用条件、申报窗口每年都会更新。请以税务部门与片区管理机构发布的最新文件为准，不要根据宣传材料估算税负。

人才政策

深圳各区对外籍与港澳台人才有工作许可便利、住房补贴、子女入学与个税补贴等安排，不同区的力度与门槛差异很大。申请通常需要用人单位作为主体提交，个人无法单独办理。

银行开户与招聘

企业开户需营业执照、法人证件、公司章程与经营场所证明，银行会做尽职调查，外籍法人建议预留数个工作日。招聘以线上平台为主，技术岗位竞争激烈，中高端人才更看重项目与股权安排而非底薪。

数据合规与知识产权

中国已建立以《网络安全法》《数据安全法》《个人信息保护法》为基础的数据合规框架，涉及个人信息出境、重要数据处理的业务需按适用规则履行评估或备案义务。

知识产权方面，建议在中国境内同步申请商标与专利——中国的商标制度实行申请在先原则，仅在境外注册不会自动获得保护。在合作协议中明确技术成果归属、竞业限制与争议解决方式，是成本最低的预防措施。

三个常见误区

  • 以为深圳只是工厂。深圳的研发投入与设计能力与制造业同样强，很多产品的定义也在这里完成。用「代工思维」谈合作，很难谈到核心团队。
  • 低估城市尺度。深圳东西跨度约 80 公里，一天安排三场跨区会议几乎必然迟到。按片区而不是按时间排日程。
  • 低估旺季订房难度。展会季与 APEC 会议周叠加时，福田与南山的高星级酒店会提前数周售罄，价格同步上浮。住宿要在确认行程的第一时间解决。

政策提示：税收优惠、产业扶持、人才补贴、外资准入与企业注册要求均可能调整，各区差异明显。请以深圳市及各区政府、税务、市场监管、出入境管理部门的官方最新文件为准。本页为独立整理的信息指南，非官方发布，也不构成税务或法律意见。

答疑

商务类常见问题

不会中文，能在深圳顺利开会吗？

可以。福田、南山的企业与外企团队里能说英语的人不少，酒店、会展中心也基本能对接英文需求。但正式谈判与宴请建议配备译员，尤其是涉及合同条款、技术细节与价格时，避免因语义偏差造成误解。

名片一定要中文吗？

不是必须，但中英文对照会明显方便对方。中文名的音译请让母语者核对，避免出现不雅或别扭的用字。名片刻上职务有助于对方判断该由谁和你对接。

深圳的商务宴请一般什么价位？

差异很大。商务简餐人均 80—150 元可以吃得不错；中高端粤菜或江浙菜宴请人均 300—800 元常见；高星级酒店包间与高档海鲜则可能上千元。多数商务宴请在 300—600 元人均区间，酒水往往是最大变量。

可以送礼物吗？送什么合适？

可以，且很受欢迎。本国特产、企业纪念品、高品质文具、茶叶、设计类小物都是安全选择。避开钟表、伞、刀具以及白色或菊花主题的物品。礼物双手递上，对方一般不当面拆开，这是习惯而非冷淡。

怎样预订会议场地？

会展中心的会议厅通常通过场馆官网或会展服务公司预订；酒店会议室直接联系酒店销售部。需要提供会议名称、人数、时长、设备需求与餐饮要求。11 月是旺季，建议提前 2—3 个月确认，并同步锁定住宿。

可以带自己的团队来做演示吗？

可以。设备方面建议自带笔记本、HDMI 与 Type-C 转换头，并准备离线版演示文件与热点。若需要同声传译、直播或录制，请提前向场馆或酒店预订，临时加装往往来不及。

在深圳开公司需要多久？

内资企业通过「一网通办」办理，材料齐备时通常数个工作日可完成设立登记。外商投资企业因涉及主体资格文件的公证认证与翻译，整体周期更长，通常需要数周。银行开户与税务登记需在执照领取后单独办理，建议预留时间。

前海的 15% 企业所得税我也能享受吗？

15% 税率适用于前海合作区内、主营业务属于《企业所得税优惠目录》范围且满足实质性运营要求的企业，并非注册在前海即可自动享受。目录、条件与申报流程以税务部门最新文件为准，建议在设立前咨询专业机构或前海管理机构。

Industry map

What Shenzhen actually makes its money from

One thing unites Shenzhen's industries: R&D, supply chain and volume manufacturing all happen within a single city radius. That is the biggest difference between Shenzhen and most other tech hubs.

Electronics and semiconductors

The city's core industry, running from chip design, displays and modules through to final assembly. In 2025 Shenzhen produced 74.77 billion integrated circuits, 45.87 million computer units and 2.30 million servers. Nanshan, Longhua and Pingshan carry most of the design and manufacturing.

New energy vehicles and intelligent mobility

Automotive manufacturing value added grew 29.4% in 2025, among the fastest of any industrial category. Vehicles, batteries, motors and controllers, smart cockpits and autonomous driving software form a complete chain across Shenzhen and its neighbours. Pingshan and the Shenshan Special Cooperation Zone are key bases.

Drones and the low-altitude economy

Shenzhen produced 7.45 million civil drones in 2025, up 40.1% year on year. It is the global centre for consumer drone R&D and manufacturing, and is now running trials in low-altitude logistics, inspection and passenger-carrying aircraft. Nanshan and Longgang are the main clusters.

AI and computing power

From chips and servers to models and applications, Shenzhen's AI sector is grafted onto its hardware supply chain. Server output grew 29.5% in 2025, and value added in information transmission, software and IT services exceeded RMB 520 billion. Computing infrastructure and policy support are priorities for every district's investment teams.

Medical devices and biomedicine

Shenzhen is strong in monitoring, imaging, in-vitro diagnostics and implantable consumables, with a mature supplier base. Pingshan is the core biomedical district, Guangming Science City focuses on basic research and large-scale research facilities, and Nanshan hosts a cluster of high-value consumables and diagnostics companies.

Finance and Qianhai

Financial sector value added reached RMB 526.16 billion in 2025, up 12.1%. The Qianhai Shenzhen-Hong Kong Modern Service Industry Cooperation Zone concentrates cross-border finance, financial leasing, supply chain finance and foreign institutions — the most open financial district in the city.

Cross-border e-commerce

Backed by ports, the airport and a mature supply chain, Shenzhen has one of the densest concentrations of cross-border sellers and logistics providers in China. In 2025 online retail by above-scale companies accounted for more than half of their goods retail sales. Qianhai, Bao'an and Longgang all host e-commerce parks.

Industrial design and fashion

Shenzhen is a UNESCO City of Design, with deep roots in industrial design, jewellery, watches, eyewear, apparel and furniture. Shuibei for gold and jewellery, Dalang for apparel and Henggang for eyewear have each formed a complete industrial belt.

Creative industries

Games, digital content, film and television production and creative design are the mainstays. Nanshan Science Park, OCT-LOFT and the Longgang digital creative corridor host a large number of content and design teams.

Meeting venues

Three tiers, three different jobs

  • Shenzhen World Exhibition & Convention CenterIn Bao'an District, an enormous venue suited to exhibitions, major launches and industry shows of tens of thousands of square metres. It sits well away from the centre, so attendees usually stay in Bao'an or arrive by metro.
  • Shenzhen Convention & Exhibition CenterIn the Futian CBD next to the Convention Center metro stop, ideal for mid-sized exhibitions, industry forums and side events. The densest hotel and restaurant cluster in the city, all within walking distance.
  • Shenzhen International Exchange CenterIn Xiangmihu North, Futian, opened in July 2026, with a conference centre, accompanying hotel and garden meeting zones. Designed for high-level international summits and government-business events, and the district hosting the APEC main venue.
  • Hotel function spaceHigh-end hotels in Futian and Nanshan generally have divisible ballrooms and boardrooms suited to 20–300 people, with catering and coffee breaks bundled in — usually the least effort.
  • Booking windowMid to late November is peak exhibition season, compounded this year by the APEC meeting week. Venues and hotels are extremely tight; secure space two to three months ahead and settle accommodation at the same time.
  • Support servicesExhibition centres and hotels can normally supply interpretation equipment, translators, registration systems, streaming and stand construction — but only if booked ahead. Last-minute arrangements are expensive and often unavailable.
One practical lesson

For meetings under about 50 people, take a boardroom in a high-end hotel rather than a small hall in an exhibition centre. The hotel bundles the room, coffee breaks, lunch and accommodation into one contract, and the coordination time you save during the meeting week is worth far more than the price difference.

Etiquette

Nine moves that shape the first impression

Business culture in Shenzhen is the least ceremonial in mainland China: efficiency first, flat hierarchies. But getting the basics right still lowers the cost of every conversation.

  1. Be on time — and five minutes early

    Punctuality is basic respect. Arriving five minutes early leaves room for cards and small talk. If you will be late, message ahead with your expected arrival time. People here are forgiving about delay itself; they are not forgiving about silence.

  2. Names, titles and business cards

    Offer and receive cards with both hands. Read the card for a moment before putting it away — do not shove it in a pocket or write on it. Titles are printed on the card, so address people as surname plus title (for example "Director Li"); when unsure, "Mr/Ms plus surname" is always safe.

  3. Meeting pace

    Small talk is brief and the agenda starts quickly. Five minutes of weather, flights and your last visit is plenty; after that, get to the subject. Off-agenda conversation belongs at the table, not in the room.

  4. Decision style

    Shenzhen companies decide quickly and lean on data. Numbers, timelines and named owners work far better than brand storytelling. If someone says "we will look into it", it usually means another internal round — follow up politely in three days.

  5. Relationships and trust

    Shenzhen is a city of migrants; networks are built through working together rather than family ties. A common rhythm is: first meeting on the deal, second on the people, third on long-term cooperation. Consistency, keeping your word and delivering on time build more trust than an extravagant dinner.

  6. Gift taboos

    Avoid clocks (homophone for attending a funeral), umbrellas (homophone for parting), anything white or chrysanthemum-themed, and knives. Specialities from home, company merchandise, good stationery or tea are all safe. Present with both hands; the recipient usually will not open it in front of you.

  7. Drinking and banquets

    Drinking culture here is milder than in many mainland cities, and tea is entirely acceptable in most settings. If someone toasts you, stand, and hold your glass slightly lower than theirs. If you cannot drink, say so plainly on health grounds and raise your tea cup — you will not normally be pressed. Never drink and drive; enforcement is strict.

  8. Serving chopsticks and shared dishes

    Chinese restaurants usually provide serving chopsticks and a serving spoon. Use them to take food, and never rummage through a shared dish with your own chopsticks. Some restaurants serve individual portions — follow the host or the server. Do not stand chopsticks upright in rice, and do not tap your bowl.

  9. Dress

    For formal business, wear a dark suit, shirt and leather shoes; a tie is optional but worth packing. Given the humid climate, business casual (collared shirt with trousers or a knee-length skirt) is accepted in most office settings. Air conditioning is cold — carry a jacket.

Banqueting

How to order, and where to sit

Ordering

  • Let the host order; as a guest you can add one or two preferences or dietary needs, but do not take over.
  • Roughly one dish per person, plus a soup and a staple. Portions in Shenzhen are generous, so order less than you think at first.
  • Ask about restrictions up front: no chilli, vegetarian, halal, seafood allergies, religious requirements. Easier than fixing it later.
  • If there are local specialities, order one or two: Cantonese roast meats, steamed sea fish, claypot congee or dim sum.
  • Use serving chopsticks. If someone serves you food it is a courtesy — you need not refuse; simply leave what you cannot finish.
  • The host normally pays; insisting on splitting the bill can cause loss of face. Thank them and offer to host next time.

Seating at a round table

  • The seat facing the door is usually the host's seat, taken by the host or the most senior person present.
  • The seat to the host's right is the first guest of honour; to the left, the second. Wait for the host to indicate before sitting.
  • The host often sits opposite the seat of honour or near the service entrance to manage dishes and the bill.
  • An interpreter usually sits just behind or beside the guest, to translate quietly.
  • If you are unsure, stand by a chair and let the host place you. That is the one approach that never goes wrong.

Travel practicalities

Things to sort out three days before

Where to stay

Futian for finance and government; Nanshan for technology and internet; Luohu or Qianhai if you cross to Hong Kong often. During the meeting week Futian and Nanshan are the tightest markets — favour flexible rates. See Where to stay.

Commuting and vehicle restrictions

Shenzhen restricts non-local licence plates on weekdays during peak hours, so check the times and area if you drive a mainland-plated car from outside the city. Weekday peaks (roughly 07:30–09:00 and 17:30–19:30) are heavy on main arteries; add 40% to the navigation estimate for any cross-district meeting.

Parking

Office towers and malls have underground parking, around RMB 10–15 for the first hour, more in the core districts. Spaces fill after 9 a.m. in busy areas, and on-street parking near venues is normally suspended during the meeting week — take the metro.

Airport and high-speed rail

Metro Line 11 runs from Bao'an International Airport to Futian in about 50 minutes; the airport express bus or a taxi takes roughly 40–70 minutes at peak. High-speed trains mostly serve Shenzhen North (Longhua) and Futian station, the latter right in the centre. See Getting around.

Printing and business centres

High-end hotels generally run a business centre for printing, binding, scanning and short-term meeting room hire, charged per page or per hour. Neighbourhood print shops are cheaper and faster, and there is usually one on the ground floor of every office tower.

Interpretation

Exhibition centres, the International Exchange Center and most high-end hotels can arrange simultaneous interpretation equipment and interpreters if booked ahead. For small meetings, a "foreign guest + native interpreter + main counterpart" format is often more efficient than full simultaneous setup.

Business cards

Print bilingual cards at home, and have a native speaker check your Chinese name and title. Local print shops can also turn around a reprint within a day — most office districts have one.

Connectivity and presentations

Venue Wi-Fi usually needs a phone number for verification, so bring your own hotspot and an offline copy of your deck. HDMI is the standard projection input and USB-C direct connection is increasingly common, but bring your own adaptor.

Timing expectations

People are punctual, but everyone accepts that traffic happens. Minimum reliable lead times for cross-district meetings: 30 minutes within Futian, 45 minutes Futian to Nanshan, 60 minutes Futian to Bao'an.

Setting up

If you want to open an office here

Company registration and foreign investment

Setting up a foreign-invested enterprise has been substantially simplified, and most filings can be submitted online through the unified government service platform. Expect to provide the investor's incorporation documents, the legal representative's identification, and proof of registered address; some documents need notarisation, legalisation and translation.

Districts such as Qianhai run dedicated windows and fast-track channels for Hong Kong and foreign companies. Follow the latest guidance from the Shenzhen market supervision authority and district service centres.

Tax incentives and industrial policy

Within the Qianhai cooperation zone, enterprises whose main business falls under the corporate income tax incentive catalogue pay tax at a reduced rate of 15%. Hetao, Guangming Science City and other districts run their own industrial support and R&D subsidy schemes.

Catalogue entries, eligibility and application windows are updated annually. Check the latest documents from the tax authority and the district authority rather than estimating your tax position from promotional material.

Talent policy

Shenzhen's districts offer foreign and Hong Kong/Macao/Taiwan talent a range of measures: work permit facilitation, housing subsidies, school placement and personal income tax subsidies. The generosity and thresholds vary sharply between districts.

Bank accounts and hiring

Corporate account opening needs the business licence, the legal representative's identification, the articles of association and proof of premises, and banks conduct due diligence — allow several working days, especially if the legal representative is foreign. Hiring runs mainly through online platforms; technical roles are competitive, and senior candidates weigh project scope and equity far more than base salary.

Data compliance and IP

China has built a data compliance framework on the Cybersecurity Law, the Data Security Law and the Personal Information Protection Law. Businesses involving outbound personal information or important data need to complete the applicable assessment or filing.

On intellectual property, file trademarks and patents in China as well — the trademark system follows first-to-file, so a foreign registration gives you no automatic protection here. Spelling out ownership of technical results, non-compete terms and dispute resolution in your agreements is the cheapest form of prevention.

Three common mistakes

  • Assuming Shenzhen is just a factory. Its R&D and design capability is as strong as its manufacturing, and many products are defined here. Approaching partners with an outsourcing mindset rarely reaches the core team.
  • Underestimating the scale. The city spans roughly 80 km east to west, so three cross-district meetings in one day will almost certainly run late. Plan by district, not by clock.
  • Underestimating peak-season hotels. When exhibition season overlaps the APEC meeting week, high-end hotels in Futian and Nanshan sell out weeks ahead and rates rise. Sort accommodation the moment your trip is confirmed.

Policy note: tax incentives, industrial support, talent subsidies, foreign investment access and company registration requirements can all change, and differ significantly between districts. Rely on the latest official documents from the Shenzhen and district governments, the tax authority, the market supervision authority and the immigration administration. Q&A

Common business questions

Can I run meetings in Shenzhen without speaking Chinese?

Yes. Plenty of people in Futian and Nanshan companies, and in foreign-invested teams, speak English, and hotels and exhibition centres can usually handle English requests. For formal negotiation and banquets, though, bring an interpreter — especially for contract clauses, technical detail and pricing, where small misreadings become expensive.

Do business cards have to be in Chinese?

Not strictly, but bilingual cards make life much easier for your counterpart. Have a native speaker check the transliteration of your name so it does not read oddly or unfortunately. Including your job title helps people work out who should be talking to whom.

What does a business meal typically cost?

It varies widely. A working lunch at RMB 80–150 per person is comfortable; mid-to-high-end Cantonese or Jiangnan cuisine for a hosted dinner commonly runs RMB 300–800 per person; hotel private rooms and premium seafood can exceed RMB 1,000. Most business dinners sit in the RMB 300–600 range, and drinks are usually the biggest variable.

Should I bring gifts? What is appropriate?

Yes, and they are welcome. Specialities from home, company merchandise, good stationery, tea or designer small objects are all safe. Avoid clocks, umbrellas, knives and anything white or chrysanthemum-themed. Present with both hands. The recipient usually will not open it in front of you — that is custom, not coldness.

How do I book a meeting venue?

Halls in the exhibition centres are usually booked through the venue's website or an exhibition services company; hotel meeting rooms are booked directly with the hotel's sales department. Be ready with the event name, expected numbers, duration, equipment needs and catering requirements. November is peak season — confirm two to three months ahead and lock in accommodation at the same time.

Can I bring my own team to present?

Yes. Bring your own laptop, HDMI and USB-C adaptors, an offline copy of the presentation and a hotspot. If you need simultaneous interpretation, streaming or recording, book it with the venue or hotel in advance — retrofitting on the day is usually impossible.

How long does it take to set up a company in Shenzhen?

Domestic companies file through the unified online service platform and, with complete documents, registration often takes only a few working days. Foreign-invested enterprises take longer overall — usually several weeks — because investor documents need notarisation, legalisation and translation. Bank account opening and tax registration are handled separately after the licence is issued, so allow extra time.

Can I benefit from Qianhai's 15% corporate income tax rate?

The 15% rate applies to enterprises in the Qianhai cooperation zone whose main business falls within the corporate income tax incentive catalogue and which meet the substantive operation requirements. Registering in Qianhai does not automatically qualify you. The catalogue, conditions and application process follow the latest tax authority documents — check with a professional adviser or the Qianhai authority before you set up.
