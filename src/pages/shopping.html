先看这一页

深圳购物地图：五个完全不同世界

不要用「深圳哪里好逛」这种问法。先确定你要买什么，再决定去哪——这五类地方的距离、价格、交易规则和所需时间完全不同。

商场：品牌、首店、餐饮一体

万象城、万象天地、海岸城、COCO Park、卓悦中心、深业上城——深圳的头部商场密度极高，购物、餐饮、展览、影院打包在一起，多数与地铁站直接连通。价格是标价，不能砍价，但常有会员折扣与信用卡活动。

老街与批发市场：东门、南油、大浪

东门老街是最老牌的步行街，人流大、价格弹性大，可以议价。南油（女装）和大浪（服装产业带）是批发逻辑：看款、问「拿货价」、谈起批量。零售客人也能买，但要有耐心。

专业市场：华强北与水贝

华强北是全球规模最大的电子元器件与消费电子市场群；水贝是全国最大的黄金珠宝集散地之一。这两处的共同点是「专业」——懂行的人能买到极好的价格，不懂行的人容易吃亏。规则见下文。

口岸与跨境：蛇口、福田口岸

深圳与香港一河之隔，福田、皇岗、深圳湾、莲塘、蛇口等口岸通香港。部分品牌与品类在香港的价格、税率、款式与内地不同，值得比价；但要注意各口岸的通关时间与携带物品的免税额度规定。

内行提示：退税是「钱」，但要提前做三件事

境外旅客在深圳购物可以办理离境退税。真正决定你能不能拿到钱的，不是买得多，而是三件小事：一、进店先问「你们是退税商店吗」（只有备案退税商店才能开退税申请单）；二、买单时出示护照并主动要求开具退税申请单——离店后再补开通常非常麻烦；三、留出足够时间在口岸办理验核，会议周期间口岸排队会比平时长。深圳的退税商店已超过 2500 家，重点商圈、景区和交通枢纽基本覆盖。

华强北：真实规则与真实风险

华强北不是一家商场，而是一片由赛格、华强电子世界等多个专业市场组成的街区，规模在全球范围内都属最大之一。这里能买到极便宜的手机配件、维修零件、数据线、充电器、小家电和二手数码，也能找到无人机、机器人和各种「只有深圳才有」的电子产品。

先说规则。

  • 先确定型号再谈价。同一款产品可能有十几个版本（国行 / 港版 / 国际版、原装 / 兼容 / 翻新）。型号说不清，价格就没有可比性。
  • 议价是常态。报价通常预留议价空间，货比三家再回头，成功率最高。批量买（例如十条数据线）单价会更低。
  • 当场验货。当面开机、检查序列号、测试充电与接口、清点配件。要求开具收据——正规档口都能开。
  • 保留包装和凭证。出境时如果被海关查验，发票是证明物品来源与价值的唯一凭据。

再说风险。华强北的复杂性在于「同一句话在不同档口含义不同」。

四个必须警惕的坑

一、翻新与拆机件冒充全新。手机、平板、耳机、相机是高发品类。价格明显低于官方渠道的「全新正品」，几乎都值得怀疑。

二、扩容机与改号机。存储容量被改写、序列号被修改的产品无法通过官方保修查验。

三、电池与充电器安全。来源不明的电池、充电宝、充电器存在过热风险，不要图便宜购买容量虚标的产品。

四、售后几乎不存在。批发市场的逻辑是「出门不认」。购买前明确退换条件，并写在小票上。

实用的做法：把华强北当成「配件与小件电子产品的市场」，而不是买贵重主设备的渠道。手机、笔记本、相机等高价商品，去官方直营店、授权店或大型商场专柜更安心。想买无人机、运动相机这类深圳本土科技品牌，优先考虑品牌官方门店或授权渠道——它们通常也在退税商店名单内。

水贝买金：金价 + 工费

水贝是全国最大的黄金珠宝集散地之一。它的价格逻辑与商场品牌金店完全不同，理解一句话就够：总价 = 当日金价 × 克重 + 工费。

项目怎么算能不能谈
金价按当天国际金价 / 上金所价格为基础，各商家报价略有差异基本不能谈，金价随行就市
工费（加工费）按克计，款式越复杂越高；素圈手镯一类最便宜可以谈，尤其非旺季、非节假日
一口价商品品牌金店常见，按件标价，折合单克价通常高于金价不能谈，溢价即品牌与设计
检测与证书可要求复称、检测；正规商铺提供票据—

工费的差别很大：素圈、素链一类工艺简单的，工费可以低到每克几元；雕花、古法金、镶嵌类工费可达每克二三十元甚至更高。有报道显示，2026 年国庆假期水贝首饰工费一度升至每克 25–35 元，节后随着客流回落而回落，素圈手镯的工费也从平日的约 5 元升到 10 元再回落。这说明工费是市场化的、波动的，砍价空间随行情变化。

买金的五条建议：

  • 先问「今天金价多少、工费多少一克」，把两项分开算总价，不要只问总价。
  • 要求当面复称（克重精确到小数点后两位），并核对票据上的克重。
  • 问清楚成色（足金 999 等）与是否可退换。
  • 不要买来路不明、没有票据的「低价金」。金价是全球公开价格，明显低于市价的报价通常另有原因。
  • 黄金饰品出境有申报与限额规定，购买大额金饰前先了解海关对贵金属出境的要求。

金价与工费每日波动，本页不给出具体价格数字作为报价，只说明计价结构。请以到店当日商家公示价格为准。

离境退税：怎么办理

境外旅客（在中国境内连续居住不超过 183 天）在备案退税商店购买个人自用物品，可申请退还增值税。核心规则如下。

项目现行规定
适用对象在中国境内连续居住不超过 183 天的境外旅客（含港澳台）
起退点同一退税商店同一天购物满 200 元人民币（2025 年由 500 元下调）
退税率适用 13% 增值税率的商品退 11%；适用 9% 增值税率的商品退 8%
离境期限一般要求在购买后一定期限内（通常 90 天内）携带物品出境；「即买即退」离境期限已统一延长至 28 天
现金退税限额由 1 万元提高至 2 万元
验核方式（2026 年 7 月 1 日起）销售额 1 万元以下的退税申请单按比例随机抽检；1 万元及以上逐单实物验核
办理方式全流程无纸化；「即买即退」异地互认，可在异地口岸办结

深圳是全国首批离境退税试点城市，目前备案退税商店已超过 2500 家，在 6 个口岸设有离境退税点，并形成了「集中退付点 + 退付商店」的「即买即退」网络，支持在购物现场或通过支付宝、微信等渠道即时到账。口岸、机场和重点商圈还布设了自助退税机，退税小程序提供 14 种语言服务。

  1. 确认商店是退税商店

    进店先问或看门口标识。只有备案退税商店能开具离境退税申请单。

  2. 购物时出示护照

    结算时出示本人护照（或港澳台通行证等有效证件），请店员开具《离境退税申请单》和销售发票。姓名必须与护照一致。

  3. 选择「即买即退」或离境时办理

    「即买即退」可以在购物现场先拿到退税款（通常需信用卡预授权或使用指定渠道）；也可以选择到口岸离境时统一办理。前者现金流更快，后者流程更简单。

  4. 保管好商品与单据

    在离境前不要拆封或使用退税商品——海关可能要求实物验核。商品、发票、申请单三样必须齐全并对应。

  5. 在口岸办理验核与退税

    留出额外时间。1 万元以下为随机抽检，1 万元及以上逐单验核。深圳已实现「一单一包一码」密封管理，办理时长大幅压缩，但会议周期间口岸人流较大，建议提前到达。

以上为 2026 年 10 月整理的公开政策要点，依据商务部等六部门 2026 年第 74 号通知（2026 年 7 月 1 日实施）。具体适用范围、单据要求与办理细节请以海关、税务部门及退税代理机构的最新公告为准。

值得带走的手信

科技产品

无人机、运动相机、智能手表手环、AI 眼镜、耳机——深圳是这些产品的全球主要产地，价格与型号选择都有优势。买贵重电子产品请走官方门店或授权渠道，并保留发票以便出境申报与退税。

食品类

沙井蚝油（本地名产，做菜实用）、南山荔枝干与荔枝酒（荔枝是深圳的地理标志农产品，鲜果 6–7 月上市，干货全年可买）、客家黄酒（娘酒，客家传统，适合炖鸡）、广式腊味与饼食。带食品出境前请确认目的地国家的动植物检疫规定。

茶与干货

广东与潮汕的乌龙茶（单丛、铁观音）、普洱茶饼、凉茶冲剂。茶叶在商场超市、茶叶专业市场和潮汕茶行都能买到；送人建议选有独立包装与生产日期的礼盒。

品牌与折扣：怎么买更划算

  • 奥特莱斯（Outlet）：深圳及周边有多个奥特莱斯与折扣商场，主要销售过季服饰、鞋包、运动品牌，折扣幅度常见但款式受限。适合买基础款和运动装备，不适合追新。
  • 商场活动：深圳商场的促销节点集中在大促与节假日（如国庆、双十一、春节前），会员日、信用卡满减、积分兑换可以叠加，能省下的比例不小。办理商场会员通常免费。
  • 香港跨境比价：部分国际品牌在港价格与款式不同，奢侈品、化妆品、药品与部分电子产品是常见比价对象。注意：香港与内地商品定价、税费结构不同，价差不固定；携带物品入境有免税额度与申报规定，请事先查清。
  • 境外旅客：别忘了退税本身就是最大的折扣——退 8%–11% 相当于直接降价，远高于大部分商场活动力度。

讲价文化与营业时间

哪里能讲价：百货商场、品牌专柜、连锁店、便利店、超市——统一定价，不能讲价，讲了也不会有结果。东门老街、华强北、水贝（工费部分）、南油与大浪（批发）、部分夜市与个体小店——可以讲价，方式温和：先问价，再说「能不能便宜点」，或者用「我买两件 / 三条」换取单价优惠。切忌一上来就砍一半，这会被理解为不认真。

营业时间：商场普遍 10:00 开门、22:00 关门，餐饮层更晚；东门等步行街 10:00 到 22:30 左右，节假日延长；华强北专业市场多数 9:30–19:00，晚上大量档口关门，不要安排夜间采购；水贝多数商铺 10:00–19:00；南油、大浪的批发市场集中在白天，下午晚些时候收摊。便利店与部分超市 24 小时营业。

移动支付与境外卡

深圳几乎全部消费场景都支持微信支付和支付宝。境外旅客可以下载 App、用境外手机号注册，并绑定 Visa、Mastercard、JCB、Diners Club、Discover 等标识的国际银行卡，然后扫码付款。几个需要知道的点：

  • 绑定需要发卡行授权，部分银行的系统可能拒绝绑定请求，此时需要联系发卡行客服或改用中国境内银行卡。
  • 平台对绑定境外卡设有交易限额（公开说明中包括单笔与年度上限）；超过一定金额的交易可能按约 3% 收取手续费。这些规则会调整，请以 App 内最新公示为准。
  • 大型商场、酒店、机场与部分餐厅支持直接刷境外卡；街边小店与夜市可能只收移动支付。
  • 随身带少量现金（几百元）作为备份，但不要依赖大额现金——很多小店找不开。

11 月的深圳该穿什么

这是本页最实用的一节。深圳 11 月的天气对北方来客是惊喜，对热带来客是凉爽，真正的问题从来不是室外，而是室内空调。

根据长期气候统计，深圳 11 月白天最高气温通常在 25°C 上下，夜间最低在 20°C 上下，历史记录中最低约 16–17°C、最高约 27°C。降雨很少，整月平均降雨量约 44 毫米，约 4 个雨天，偶有单日强降雨。也就是说：白天温和、早晚偏凉、几乎不下雨、但可能偶尔来一场急雨。

常见问题

在深圳买东西需要给小费吗？

不需要。商场、餐厅、出租车都不收小费。部分高端餐厅会在账单中加收约 10% 服务费，这是明确标注的，不是小费。

华强北能买到正品 iPhone 或相机吗？

能买到，但风险高于官方渠道。华强北的核心优势是配件、元件和维修，而不是贵重主设备。购买手机、笔记本、相机等高价商品，建议优先官方直营店、授权店或大型商场专柜；如确在华强北购买，务必当面验机、核对序列号、索取带明细的发票。

水贝买金比品牌店便宜多少？

便宜的部分主要来自工费与品牌溢价，而不是金价本身——金价是公开的、全球联动的。同一件足金首饰，水贝的「金价 + 工费」结构通常比品牌店的「一口价」更透明。具体差额随金价、款式与行情波动，无法给出固定数字。

离境退税需要多少购物金额？

同一退税商店同一天购物满 200 元人民币即可申请（2025 年由 500 元下调）。退税率取决于商品适用的增值税率：13% 税率商品退 11%，9% 税率商品退 8%。现金退税限额已提高至 2 万元。

可以用境外信用卡直接付款吗？

大型商场、酒店、机场和部分餐厅可以。街边小店、夜市与部分老店通常只支持微信 / 支付宝，因此建议提前绑定境外卡到微信或支付宝，并随身带少量现金。绑定可能被发卡行拒绝，也可能有单笔和年度限额，请以 App 内说明为准。

11 月需要带羽绒服吗？

通常不需要。11 月深圳白天 25°C 上下、夜间 20°C 上下，最冷的历史记录也在 16°C 以上。真正需要的是分层：一件可穿脱的薄外套或针织衫，加一件用于会议室的西装外套。如果行程包括夜间海边，加一件挡风的薄风衣更实用。

会议周期间购物要注意什么？

核心区（尤其福田会展中心周边）人流与交通压力上升，商场与热门餐厅排队更长；口岸办理退税与通关的时间也可能延长。建议把大额购物安排在会议前后，预留往返时间，随身携带护照，并提前确认商店的退税资质。

Shenzhen's shopping map: five different worlds

Do not ask "where is good for shopping in Shenzhen". Decide what you want to buy first, then pick the place — these five categories differ completely in distance, price, transaction rules and how much time they need.

Malls: brands, flagships, food and drink

MixC, MixC World, Coastal City, COCO Park, One Avenue and UpperHills — Shenzhen's top malls are densely packed and bundle retail, dining, exhibitions and cinemas, mostly connected directly to metro stations. Prices are fixed and haggling does not work, but member discounts and card promotions are common.

Old streets and wholesale: Dongmen, Nanyou, Dalang

Dongmen is the oldest pedestrian shopping street: heavy footfall, flexible prices, bargaining expected. Nanyou (women's fashion) and Dalang (the garment belt) work on wholesale logic — look at the styles, ask the wholesale price, discuss quantities. Retail buyers are welcome but need patience.

Specialist markets: Huaqiangbei and Shuibei

Huaqiangbei is one of the world's largest clusters of electronic components and consumer electronics. Shuibei is one of China's largest gold and jewellery distribution hubs. Both reward knowledge and punish carelessness — see the rules below.

Borders and cross-border: Shekou, Futian

Shenzhen sits across a river from Hong Kong, with crossings at Futian, Huanggang, Shenzhen Bay, Liantang and Shekou. Some brands and categories differ from the mainland in price, tax and range, so comparing is worthwhile — but check each port's opening hours and the duty-free allowances for what you carry back.

Insider tip: the tax refund is real money — do three things first

Overseas visitors can claim a departure tax refund on purchases in Shenzhen. What decides whether you actually get the money is not how much you spend but three small habits. First, ask on entering whether the shop is a refund store — only registered stores can issue a refund application form. Second, show your passport at checkout and ask for the form; getting one issued after you leave is usually painful. Third, allow real time at the port for verification, since queues run longer than usual during the meeting week. Shenzhen now has more than 2,500 registered refund stores, covering the main commercial districts, attractions and transport hubs.

Huaqiangbei: the real rules and the real risks

Huaqiangbei is not one mall but a district of specialist markets — SEG, Huaqiang Electronics World and others — and one of the largest such clusters anywhere. You can buy extremely cheap phone accessories, repair parts, cables, chargers, small appliances and second-hand electronics, and find drones, robots and all kinds of products that exist only because Shenzhen exists.

The rules first.

  • Pin down the exact model before you talk price. One product may come in a dozen versions — mainland, Hong Kong, international, genuine, compatible, refurbished. Without a precise model there is nothing to compare.
  • Bargaining is normal. Opening quotes leave room. Compare several stalls and come back; that works best. Buying in quantity, say ten cables, lowers the unit price.
  • Inspect on the spot. Power it on, check the serial number, test charging and ports, count the accessories. Ask for a receipt — legitimate stalls can provide one.
  • Keep the packaging and paperwork. If customs checks your bags on departure, the invoice is your only proof of origin and value.

Now the risks. The complication is that the same words mean different things at different stalls.

Four traps worth knowing

One: refurbished or stripped units sold as new. Phones, tablets, earphones and cameras are the high-risk categories. A "brand new genuine" item priced well below official channels almost always deserves suspicion.

Two: capacity-modified and serial-altered devices. Storage rewritten or serial numbers changed — these fail official warranty verification.

Three: battery and charger safety. Batteries, power banks and chargers of unknown origin carry overheating risk. Do not buy cheap units with inflated capacity claims.

Four: after-sales barely exists. The wholesale logic is "once it leaves the counter, it's yours". Agree the return conditions up front and have them written on the receipt.

The practical approach: treat Huaqiangbei as a market for accessories and small electronics, not as a channel for expensive primary devices. For phones, laptops and cameras, official stores, authorised dealers or large mall counters are far safer. For drones and action cameras from Shenzhen's own tech brands, use the brand's official or authorised channels — these are usually also registered tax refund stores.

Buying gold in Shuibei: price plus making fee

Shuibei is one of China's largest gold and jewellery distribution hubs, and its pricing logic is completely different from a branded mall counter. One sentence covers it: total price = the day's gold price × weight + making fee.

Making fees vary widely. Simple designs such as plain bangles and plain chains can carry a fee of only a few yuan per gram; engraved, heritage-style or stone-set pieces can reach 20–30 yuan per gram or more. Reporting shows that during the 2026 National Day holiday, jewellery making fees in Shuibei rose to roughly 25–35 yuan per gram, then fell back once the crowds thinned, while plain bangles went from about 5 yuan to 10 yuan and back. The lesson is that the making fee is market-driven and moves, so the room to negotiate depends on conditions.

Five rules for buying gold:

  • Ask for the day's gold price and the per-gram making fee separately, and compute the total yourself.
  • Ask to see the piece re-weighed in front of you, and check the weight on the receipt.
  • Confirm the purity (such as pure gold 999) and the return or exchange conditions.
  • Avoid "cheap gold" with no receipt or traceable origin. The gold price is globally public; a quote well below market usually has a reason.
  • Carrying gold jewellery out of China involves declaration and allowance rules. Check customs requirements before buying a large amount.

Gold prices and making fees move daily, so this page explains the pricing structure rather than quoting a price. Confirm the day's posted rates in store.

Departure tax refund: how it works

Overseas visitors who have not resided in China for more than 183 consecutive days can claim a VAT refund on personal items bought at registered refund stores. The core rules are below.

ItemCurrent rule
Who qualifiesOverseas visitors, including Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan residents, staying in mainland China for no more than 183 consecutive days
Minimum purchase200 yuan or more in a single day at the same refund store (lowered from 500 yuan in 2025)
Refund rate11% for goods carrying 13% VAT; 8% for goods carrying 9% VAT
Departure windowGoods must generally be taken out within a set period (commonly 90 days); the "refund on purchase" departure window has been unified and extended to 28 days
Cash refund limitRaised from 10,000 yuan to 20,000 yuan
Verification (from 1 July 2026)Refund applications under 10,000 yuan are subject to random sampling; those of 10,000 yuan and above are physically verified one by one
ProcessFully paperless; "refund on purchase" is mutually recognised across regions, so departure formalities can be completed at another port

Shenzhen was among China's first pilot cities for departure tax refunds. It now has more than 2,500 registered refund stores, refund counters at six ports, and a "refund on purchase" network combining central payout points with refund shops, with funds reaching shoppers on the spot or through Alipay and WeChat. Self-service refund machines are installed at ports, the airport and key commercial districts, and the refund mini-program offers service in 14 languages.

  1. Check that the store is a refund store

    Ask on entering, or look for the sign. Only registered refund stores can issue a departure tax refund application form.

  2. Show your passport when you buy

    At checkout, present your own passport (or the relevant travel permit) and ask for the refund application form and the sales invoice. The name must match your passport exactly.

  3. Choose "refund on purchase" or refund at departure

    With refund on purchase you receive the money at the shop, usually against a credit card pre-authorisation or via a designated channel. Alternatively you can handle everything at the port when you leave. The first is faster in cash terms; the second is simpler procedurally.

  4. Keep the goods and the paperwork intact

    Do not open or use refunded goods before departure — customs may ask for physical verification. The item, invoice and application form must all be present and match.

  5. Complete verification and refund at the port

    Allow extra time. Applications under 10,000 yuan are sampled randomly; 10,000 yuan and above are verified individually. Shenzhen's sealed "one form, one bag, one code" system has cut processing time sharply, but port traffic is heavy during the meeting week — arrive early.

Souvenirs worth carrying home

Technology

Drones, action cameras, smartwatches and bands, AI glasses, earphones — Shenzhen is a primary global production base for these, with an advantage in both price and range. Buy expensive electronics through official or authorised channels and keep the invoice for customs and tax refund purposes.

Food

Shajing oyster sauce (a genuine local product and useful in any kitchen), Nanshan dried lychee and lychee wine (lychee is a protected local agricultural product; fresh fruit comes in June–July, dried is available year-round), Hakka rice wine (niang jiu, traditionally used to stew chicken), Cantonese cured meats and pastries. Guangdong and Chaoshan oolong (dancong, tieguanyin), pu'er cakes, herbal tea sachets. Buy from malls and supermarkets, specialist tea markets or Chaoshan tea shops. For gifts, choose boxed sets with individual packaging and a production date.

Brands and discounts: how to pay less

  • Outlets: Shenzhen and its surroundings have several outlet and discount malls selling previous-season clothing, shoes, bags and sportswear. Discounts are real but the range is limited — good for basics and sports gear, not for the latest releases.
  • Mall promotions: Shenzhen malls concentrate their promotions around major sales and holidays (National Day, Double Eleven, before Lunar New Year). Member days, card rebates and points can be stacked, and the savings add up. Mall membership is usually free to join.
  • Comparing with Hong Kong: some international brands differ in price and range across the border; luxury goods, cosmetics, medicines and some electronics are the common comparisons. Note that pricing and tax structures differ and the gap is not fixed, and that there are duty-free allowances and declaration rules for what you bring back — check before you shop.
  • Overseas visitors: do not forget that the tax refund itself is the biggest discount available — 8% to 11% back beats most mall promotions.

Bargaining culture and opening hours

Where bargaining works: department stores, brand counters, chain stores, convenience stores and supermarkets use fixed pricing — asking will get you nowhere. Dongmen, Huaqiangbei, Shuibei (the making fee), Nanyou and Dalang (wholesale), and some night markets and independent shops are open to it, in a mild way: ask the price, then ask whether there is a discount, or offer to buy two or three for a better unit price. Do not open by halving the quote — that reads as unserious.

Opening hours: malls generally open at 10:00 and close at 22:00, with dining floors later. Pedestrian streets such as Dongmen run about 10:00–22:30 and later on holidays. Most Huaqiangbei markets run about 9:30–19:00, and many stalls shut in the evening — do not plan a night run there. Most Shuibei shops run about 10:00–19:00. Nanyou and Dalang wholesale markets operate during the day and wind down in the late afternoon. Convenience stores and some supermarkets are open 24 hours.

Mobile payment and foreign cards

WeChat Pay and Alipay are accepted almost everywhere in Shenzhen. Overseas visitors can download the apps, register with a foreign phone number, and link an international bank card bearing the Visa, Mastercard, JCB, Diners Club or Discover logo, then pay by QR code. A few points to know:

  • Linking requires authorisation from your issuing bank, and some banks reject the request because their systems cannot process it. In that case contact the bank, or use a mainland Chinese card.
  • The platforms apply transaction limits to linked foreign cards, including per-transaction and annual caps in their published guidance. A service fee of about 3% may apply above a certain amount. These rules change — check inside the app.
  • Large malls, hotels, the airport and some restaurants accept international cards directly; small street shops and night markets may accept mobile payment only.
  • Carry a small amount of cash as backup, but do not rely on large notes — many small shops cannot make change.

Insider tip: one light jacket beats three heavy ones

The biggest temperature gap in Shenzhen in November is not outdoors — it is between 26°C on the street and 22°C in the meeting room. Air conditioning in convention centres, hotel ballrooms and conference rooms runs cold year-round, and anyone indoors for six hours or more will feel it. The fix is simple: a neutral, packable blazer or cardigan, plus shoes you have already broken in. You will walk more than you expect — metro transfers and malls add up to thousands of steps, and new shoes are a classic mistake.

Typhoon and rain backup. Guangdong's typhoon season is concentrated from June to October; a November typhoon is unusual but possible, and cold fronts can still bring rain and a drop in temperature. Check the Shenzhen Meteorological Observatory forecast at weather.sz.gov.cn before you travel, carry a light folding umbrella, and put outdoor plans such as Dapeng, Nan'ao and the coastal boardwalk on your best-weather day. Indoor alternatives include museums, art galleries, malls and the area around the convention centre.

Laundry and dry cleaning. Mid-range and upscale hotels generally offer laundry and dry cleaning, usually with same-day pickup and delivery for a fee, and some have self-service launderettes. Chain laundry shops in malls can press a shirt quickly. Demand peaks during the meeting week, so send items a day ahead and confirm the collection time.

Where to buy clothes in an emergency: basics brands such as Uniqlo and Muji have stores in most Shenzhen malls, with stable prices and full size runs — ideal for topping up on shirts, jackets, underwear and socks. Sportswear brands (in malls or outlets) are good for walking shoes. For formal wear, head to upscale malls such as MixC or One Avenue, or ask your hotel concierge for tailoring and ready-to-wear recommendations. Nanyou and Dalang offer wholesale logic with more choice and better prices, but they do not suit a business traveller in a hurry.

Frequently asked

Should I tip when shopping in Shenzhen?

No. Malls, restaurants and taxis do not expect tips. Some upscale restaurants add about 10% service charge to the bill, clearly stated — that is a service charge, not a tip.

Can I buy a genuine iPhone or camera in Huaqiangbei?

It is possible, but riskier than official channels. Huaqiangbei's strength is accessories, components and repairs rather than expensive primary devices. For phones, laptops and cameras, prefer official stores, authorised dealers or large mall counters. If you do buy there, inspect the device in person, verify the serial number and insist on an itemised invoice.

How much cheaper is gold in Shuibei than in a branded store?

The saving comes mainly from the making fee and brand premium, not the gold price, which is public and globally linked. For the same piece of pure gold, Shuibei's "gold price plus making fee" structure is usually more transparent than a branded store's fixed price. The exact difference moves with the gold price, the design and market conditions, so no fixed figure can be given.

How much do I need to spend to claim a tax refund?

200 yuan or more in a single day at the same refund store (lowered from 500 yuan in 2025). The refund rate depends on the VAT rate applied to the goods: 11% for goods at 13% VAT, 8% for goods at 9% VAT. The cash refund limit has been raised to 20,000 yuan.

Can I pay directly with a foreign credit card?

In large malls, hotels, the airport and some restaurants, yes. Small street shops, night markets and some older restaurants usually accept only WeChat Pay or Alipay, so link your foreign card in advance and carry a little cash. Linking may be declined by your issuing bank and is subject to per-transaction and annual limits — check the app's own guidance.

Do I need a down jacket in November?

Normally no. Daytime temperatures hover around 25°C and nights around 20°C, and even the coldest recorded November temperatures stay above 16°C. What you need is layering: a light jacket or knit you can add and remove, plus a blazer for meeting rooms. If your plans include the coast at night, a thin windproof coat is more useful.

What should I keep in mind for shopping during the meeting week?

Expect heavier traffic and crowds in the core districts, especially around the Futian convention area, longer queues at malls and popular restaurants, and longer processing at ports for both tax refunds and immigration. Do major shopping before or after the meeting, allow extra travel time, carry your passport, and confirm a store's refund registration in advance.
