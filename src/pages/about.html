2026 年 11 月 18–19 日，亚太经合组织（APEC）第三十三次领导人非正式会议在深圳举行，APEC CEO Summit 于 11 月 17–18 日举行。这是中国第三次主办 APEC，也是深圳第一次承办这一级别的会议。会议本身有大量官方材料，但一个来访者真正的困难往往在日常层面：地铁怎么坐、11 月穿什么、早茶怎么点、商务宴请该坐哪、手机支付能不能用境外卡。

    把「食」「住」「行」「购与衣」当成工具页，不需要通读，按当天的具体问题去查对应段落即可。商务与会议页适合会前读一遍，服务页适合落地后遇到具体事务时查。

编辑方法

本站的内容整理遵循四条自定规则。

  • 只写可以核对的信息。价格、票价、时刻、政策、统计数据等会变化的数字，必须先查证再写，并在文中标注参考年份（例如「2025 年数据」「2026 年 10 月整理」）。
  • 数字标注年份。凡是统计数字，都会写明是哪一年的；没有年份的数字，宁可写成区间或趋势，也不假装精确。
  • 无法核实的就提示核实。如果某项信息变动太快或来源不可靠，我们会直接说明，并告诉你去哪个官方渠道确认，而不是给一个看起来专业的假答案。
  • 不编造具体店名、地址与电话。餐饮与购物页面只写区域、类型和判断方法，因为具体店家、招牌与营业状态变化太快，写死了反而会误导。需要找店时，请用大众点评、美团等本地平台按区域与菜式检索，并查看近期评价。

内容以简体中文为主撰写，再翻译为同等信息量的英文。英文不是逐字翻译，而是按国际读者的阅读习惯重写，两者的信息量、结构与链接保持一致。

不要用本站的数字去签合同、赶飞机或判断法律义务。本站的数字是「帮助你做计划」的量级参考，不是实时数据。真正需要精确的时刻表、票价、签证与入境政策、退税规则，请以官方渠道的当日发布为准——下文的清单就是为此准备的。另外，价格类信息请把「参考区间」当成参考区间，不要当成报价。

本站是官方指南吗？

不是。本站是独立整理的信息指南，与 APEC 秘书处、各级政府及会议主办方没有隶属或授权关系。所有官方信息请以 sz.gov.cn、apec2026.cn 等官方渠道为准。

为什么页面里没有具体店名和地址？

因为餐厅、商场与店铺的营业状态、招牌和价格变化很快，写死具体店名与地址最容易变成过期误导。本站改为提供区域、类型、价格带与判断方法，再由你用大众点评、美团等本地平台按条件检索并查看近期评价。这也是本站「不编造精确数字」原则的一部分。

不可以。它们是为预算和计划提供的量级参考，并标注了整理时间。票价、时刻、政策与营业时间请以官方渠道当日发布为准。

不会。本站没有注册、表单、评论或分析工具，不使用 Cookie，也不加载任何第三方资源。你选择的语言只保存本机的 localStorage 中，用于记住你的偏好。

可以。本站是纯静态 HTML/CSS/JS，没有外部依赖。把整个站点目录保存到本机，双击任意页面即可在浏览器中打开阅读，无需联网。

What this site is

A city manual you can actually use

On 18–19 November 2026, the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting takes place in Shenzhen, with the APEC CEO Summit on 17–18 November. It is China's third time hosting APEC and Shenzhen's first time hosting an event at this level. The meeting itself generates plenty of official material, but the real difficulty for a visitor is usually everyday: how the metro works, what to wear in November, how to order morning tea, where to sit at a business dinner, whether a foreign card works with mobile payment.

This site is written for those specific questions. The aim is not to turn Shenzhen into a brochure but to make it the most-thumbed few pages of the book — concrete, checkable, and directly actionable.

Who it is for

APEC delegates and accompanying staff

Need to grasp meeting-week commuting, credentials, dress codes, dining and business etiquette quickly, with little margin for error.

Business travellers and investors

Care about industry, industrial parks, transport efficiency, where to stay, payments and government procedures — and want checklists rather than impressions.

Tourists and residents

Want to know where to eat, where to shop, what to do at the weekend and how to deal with people here. What they need is local common sense.

How to use it: recommended reading order

If you have half an hour, read in this order and you will get 80% of the useful information.

  1. Start with the APEC 2026 hub

    Establish the dates, theme, organisers and related arrangements, and how the meeting week affects transport, accommodation and movement. Everything else on the site sits on that background.

  2. Then read the city overview

    Learn where Shenzhen is, how big it is, which districts make it up and what industries sustain it. Every later "where to eat, where to shop, how to get there" hangs off this map.

  3. Then the itineraries

    If you have only a day or two, follow a one-day or half-day route rather than picking attractions out of a comment thread.

  4. Finally, look up food, stay, transport and shopping as needed

    Treat Food, Stay, Getting Around and Shopping as tool pages — no need to read them through. Search the section that answers today's question. Read Business & Meetings before a meeting, and Everyday & Government Services when something concrete comes up on the ground.

How we edit

Four self-imposed rules govern the content.

  • Only write what can be checked. Prices, fares, timetables, policies and statistics are verified before they are written, and the reference year is stated in the text (for example "2025 data" or "compiled October 2026").
  • Every figure carries a year. Statistical figures state the year they refer to. Where no reliable year exists, we would rather give a range or a trend than fake precision.
  • If it cannot be verified, say so. Where information changes too fast or the source is weak, we say that plainly and point you to the official channel, rather than offering a plausible-looking invention.
  • No invented shop names, addresses or phone numbers. The food and shopping pages give areas, categories and methods of judgement, because individual venues, signs and opening status change too quickly — pinning them down would mislead. To find a specific place, search by area and cuisine on local platforms such as Dianping or Meituan and read recent reviews.

Content is written first in Simplified Chinese and then rendered into English with the same substance. The English is not a literal translation: it is rewritten for international readers while keeping the same information, structure and links.

Insider tip: where this site is most likely to be misused

Do not use our figures to sign a contract, catch a flight or judge a legal obligation. They are order-of-magnitude references to help you plan, not live data. For timetables, fares, visas, entry policy and tax refund rules, rely on the official channel's publication for that day — the list below exists for exactly this. And treat any price range as a range, never as a quotation.

Corrections and feedback

We make mistakes, especially with figures that move. If you find an error, the most effective response is not to tell us but to confirm it through official channels first, because those channels are always more current than this site. Once confirmed, you are welcome to send us the name and date of the official page, and we will revise accordingly.

Frequently asked

Is this an official guide?

No. This is an independently compiled information guide with no affiliation with or authorisation from the APEC Secretariat, any level of government or the event organisers. For all official information, rely on official channels such as sz.gov.cn and apec2026.cn.

Why are there no specific shop names or addresses?

Because restaurants, malls and shops change status, signage and prices quickly, and pinning down specific names and addresses is the fastest way to become outdated and misleading. Instead we give areas, categories, price bands and ways to judge, and leave the actual search to you on local platforms such as Dianping or Meituan, where you can read recent reviews. This is also part of our rule against inventing precise numbers.

Can I rely on the prices and times on this site?

No. They are order-of-magnitude references for budgeting and planning, and each page states when it was compiled. Fares, timetables, policies and opening hours should be confirmed against the official channel's publication for the day.

Does this site collect my information?

No. There is no registration, form, comment facility or analytics, no cookies and no third-party resources. Your language choice is stored only in your browser's localStorage, to remember your preference.

Can I read it offline?

Yes. This is a purely static HTML/CSS/JS site with no external dependencies. Save the site folder to your device and double-click any page to read it in a browser, with no internet connection needed.

How often is the content updated?

The site is updated by version. The current version is the October 2026 edition, which matches the information cut-off date shown in the footer. If important policy or scheduling changes occur around the meeting week, check official channels until a new version is published.
