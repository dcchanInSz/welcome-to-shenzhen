为什么是深圳Why Shenzhen

四十多年，从小镇到世界级城市From a border town to a world city in one generation

1980 年，这里成为中国第一个经济特区。今天，它是一座人口超过 1800 万、以科技创新和制造业闻名的超大城市，也是中国对外开放最直接的一扇窗。 In 1980 this was China's first Special Economic Zone. Today it is a megacity of more than 18 million people, known for technology and manufacturing, and one of the country's most open windows to the world.

中国第一个经济特区China's first SEZ

1979 年设市，1980 年设立经济特区。「敢闯敢试」不是口号，是这座城市四十多年来的默认设置。Founded as a city in 1979 and designated a Special Economic Zone in 1980. Experimentation is not a slogan here — it is the default setting.

创新与制造同城Innovation meets manufacturing

电子信息、新能源汽车、无人机、医疗器械、人工智能。从一颗芯片到一台整机，往往在同一个都市圈内完成。Electronics, new-energy vehicles, drones, medical devices, AI. From a chip to a finished product, the supply chain often lives inside one metropolitan area.

山海与候鸟Mountains, sea, migratory birds

约 260 公里海岸线、梧桐山、大鹏半岛。深圳湾是全球候鸟迁飞路线上的重要驿站，冬天能看到黑脸琵鹭。Roughly 260 km of coastline, Wutong Mountain and the Dapeng peninsula. Shenzhen Bay is a key stop on a global migratory flyway — spoonbills winter here.

连接香港与大湾区Gateway to the Greater Bay Area

16 个口岸、深中通道、高铁网络。从深圳到香港最快十几分钟，到广州约半小时，到中山约 30 分钟。16 border crossings, the Shenzhen–Zhongshan link, high-speed rail. Hong Kong is minutes away, Guangzhou about half an hour, Zhongshan roughly 30 minutes.

时区与电源Time & power

全中国统一使用北京时间 UTC+8，不实行夏令时。电压 220V / 50Hz，插座以国标两脚与三脚为主，建议带转换头。China runs on a single time zone, UTC+8, with no daylight saving. Power is 220V / 50Hz with type A/I sockets — bring an adapter.

一条时间线A short timeline

  • 1989亚太经济合作组织成立，秘书处设于新加坡。APEC is established; its secretariat is based in Singapore.
  • 2001中国首次主办 APEC，领导人非正式会议在上海举行。China hosts APEC for the first time; the Leaders' Meeting is held in Shanghai.
  • 2014中国第二次主办，会议在北京举行。China hosts APEC a second time, in Beijing.
  • 2026APEC「中国年」，全年约 300 场活动分布在北京、上海、广州、深圳、大连、南京、苏州、杭州、成都以及香港、澳门等地。The APEC "China Year": roughly 300 events across Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Dalian, Nanjing, Suzhou, Hangzhou, Chengdu, Hong Kong and Macao.
  • 11-17 → 18亚太经合组织工商领导人峰会在深圳举行，由中国贸促会主办，主题为「促进共同繁荣，开创共赢未来」。The APEC CEO Summit takes place in Shenzhen, hosted by CCPIT under the theme "promoting prosperity together for a win-win future".
  • 11-18 → 19亚太经合组织第三十三次领导人非正式会议在深圳举行。The 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting is held in Shenzhen.
